# Scientific product roadmap

This roadmap grades named product families, not file extensions. A working TIFF, HDF5, NetCDF, Zarr, or GDAL read is reusable evidence, but it does not qualify another product's catalog identity, grid, calibration, quality flags, access route, or output provenance.

Grades describe the evidence in this repository on 2026-10-25:

- **A — qualified:** an exact product profile has a real-source, bounded, independently checked workflow documented in the repository.
- **B — existing reader plus profile:** likely supportable by an existing physical reader; product semantics and real-source acceptance remain required.
- **C — contained extension:** needs a bounded reader, data-type, or coordinate extension, followed by independent product acceptance.
- **D — different model/format:** needs a fundamentally different observation model or physical format.
- **E — access or representation unresolved:** no safe bounded route or suitable native representation is established. Discovery alone does not raise the grade.

The implementation order favors broad reader reuse, stable free access, explicit science semantics, and independent references. “Raster,” “swath,” “point/track,” “vector,” and “multidimensional” identify the scientific data model that acquisition must preserve.

Wave 4 is complete at the [geographic scientific gate](wave4/qualification.md): NISAR HHHH geographic acquisition on two inspected 10/20 m grids, separate northern-polar SMAP AM/PM, and GOES-19 mesoscale-1 C02. It does not qualify full-disk or L1b radiance. Other diagonal channels remain separate candidates, and complex covariance remains a different representation.

The next recommended milestone is [one bounded ATL06 v007 beam/segment observation slice](wave4/next-observations.md), with exact IDs, geolocation, source indices, time epochs, quality and table/track output. ATL08 and GEDI are investigated in that note but remain unimplemented. No observation is to be modeled as a raster merely to reuse the current writer.
