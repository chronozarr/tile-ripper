# Scientific product roadmap

This roadmap grades named product families, not file extensions. A working TIFF, HDF5, NetCDF, Zarr, or GDAL read is reusable evidence, but it does not qualify another product's catalog identity, grid, calibration, quality flags, access route, or output provenance.

Grades describe the evidence in this repository on 2026-10-24:

- **A — qualified:** an exact product profile has a real-source, bounded, independently checked workflow documented in the repository.
- **B — existing reader plus profile:** likely supportable by an existing physical reader; product semantics and real-source acceptance remain required.
- **C — contained extension:** needs a bounded reader, data-type, or coordinate extension, followed by independent product acceptance.
- **D — different model/format:** needs a fundamentally different observation model or physical format.
- **E — access or representation unresolved:** no safe bounded route or suitable native representation is established. Discovery alone does not raise the grade.

The implementation order favors broad reader reuse, stable free access, explicit science semantics, and independent references. “Raster,” “swath,” “point/track,” “vector,” and “multidimensional” identify the scientific data model that acquisition must preserve.

The next three highest-value qualifications are:

1. **GOES ABI, one bounded L2 family first.** Frequent public NOAA observations and prior native HDF5 slices offer broad weather value. The remaining gate is fixed-grid navigation, space/limb missingness, product DQF, exact time identity and ordinary discovery/output. NOAA explains the file-provided scanning angles and projection parameters in its [ABI fixed-grid navigation guide](https://www.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/atmospheric-composition-training/satellite_data_goes_imager_projection.php). This remains C until independent real-source geographic selection succeeds.
2. **SMAP SPL3SMP_E v6 soil moisture plus retrieval QA.** The current [NSIDC v6 guide](https://nsidc.org/data/documentation/smap-enhanced-l3-radiometer-global-and-polar-grid-daily-9-km-ease-grid-soil-moisture-version-6-user) makes an exact version available to scope. EASE-Grid, AM/PM observation identity, coordinate selection and NSIDC transport remain contained but unqualified extensions (C). Keep L4 separate.
3. **NISAR GCOV, one real-valued diagonal covariance term.** High scientific value and existing SAR/Float32/HDF5 components justify a bounded trial. ASF documents [current collection and HDF5/Harmony access](https://nisar-docs.asf.alaska.edu/earthdata-search/) and [planned versus completed services](https://hyp3-docs.asf.alaska.edu/nisar-docs/roadmap/). Inspect actual current collection maturity and an asset before admission. A generated COG or visualization is a distinct representation; complex covariance and cross-frequency/polarization cubes remain beyond this narrow trial (C for a diagonal, D for a richer model).

ICESat-2 ATL06/ATL08 and GEDI L2A/L2B remain valuable D priorities after a deliberate beam/segment/shot table contract. Native HDF5 decoding alone does not preserve their irregular footprints or along-track topology. ATL03 is a larger photon/segment-join problem; it should follow the segment model. None is newly supported by this roadmap update.

Every attempted Wave 2 family reached an A gate for the exact specimens, bands, processing versions and source routes in [Wave 2 qualification](wave2/qualification.md). Other dates, bands and processing releases retain their explicit evidence boundaries. AlphaEarth is a separately qualified bounded prototype and is not counted as an ordinary named-profile family in this 25-row matrix; its [follow-up assessment](../alphaearth/workflow.md#wave-2-follow-up-assessment) remains implementation work for a later milestone.
